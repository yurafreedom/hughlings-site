01 / The missing context

Three failed
medicines.
But what happened
in between?

The history that would show this is scattered across years of notes.

A patient is called treatment-resistant after several antidepressants fail. Sometimes the dose was too low, the course too short, or treatment was stopped early.

Reconstructing that history by hand takes time no one has.

04 / Why now

The rules already ask
for this document.

Before the most resource-intensive escalation steps, reimbursement rules require a documented treatment history. Today psychiatrists assemble it by hand.

FIFinland / Kela 3062

A documented history.
A written statement.

Esketamine reimbursement requires a specialist statement and documented prior treatments, alongside severity and other criteria.

Read the Kela criteria ↗
SESweden / Region Skåne

Every approach.
Every relevant detail.

Regional guidance asks for at least four previous treatment approaches. The referral template captures drug, dose, duration and concentration measurements.

Read the regional guidance ↗

The rule is the demand; esketamine is only the example.
Hughlings documents readiness. The clinical decision stays with the clinician.

05 / How we buildTrust is in the details

Informs clinical
management.
Does not drive it.

01

The clinician decides.

Hughlings does not prescribe, recommend or deliver treatment. The psychiatrist's decision is a separate, signed record.

02

Rules, not guesses.

Adequacy and exposure verdicts come from deterministic rules with named sources. A language model may explain a verdict; it never makes one.

03

Every output is auditable.

Each review records the exact input, the rule-set version and the time it was made. The same input and rule version produce the same result.

04

Designed for oversight.

We design for MDR and lifecycle oversight from day one, with a narrow intended use. Hughlings has not been clinically validated or CE-marked.

06 / Where we fit

Built on top,
not instead.

Medication safety is already national infrastructure. Evidence assistants answer clinical questions. Hughlings is building the treatment-history review layer between them.

We don't build interaction databases, pharmacogenomic reports, safety alerts or literature search. We build on them.
